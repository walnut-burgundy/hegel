# Canonical primary texts — Jacques Lacan

Use these as the primary textual shelf for this branch. Lacan’s seminars and published writings differ by period, editorial history, and translation. Record seminar/book, session or essay, edition, and translator for close claims.

## Core sequence

1. **“The Mirror Stage as Formative of the I Function”** in _Écrits_ — ego formation, identification, image, alienation, and misrecognition.
2. **“The Function and Field of Speech and Language in Psychoanalysis”** in _Écrits_ — symbolic order, speech, language, history, and the subject.
3. **“The Subversion of the Subject and the Dialectic of Desire in the Freudian Unconscious”** in _Écrits_ — desire, demand, lack, the Other, and the divided subject.
4. **_The Seminar, Book VII: The Ethics of Psychoanalysis_** (1959–60) — desire, the Thing, enjoyment, sublimation, ethics, and tragedy.
5. **_The Seminar, Book XI: The Four Fundamental Concepts of Psychoanalysis_** (1964) — unconscious, repetition, transference, drive, gaze, and objet petit a.
6. **_The Seminar, Book XVII: The Other Side of Psychoanalysis_** (1969–70) — the four discourses, master, knowledge, surplus-enjoyment, and social bond.

## Branch question

Use Lacan here to test where desire diverges from stated demand, where identity is mediated by an image or symbolic position, where repetition persists despite conscious intention, and what remainder prevents a person or explanation from becoming fully self-identical.
