# Canonical primary texts — Melanie Klein

Use these as the primary textual shelf for this branch. Prefer authoritative collected editions.

## Core sequence

1. **_The Psycho-Analysis of Children_** (1932) — play technique, early anxiety, phantasy, aggression, and the structure of the child’s internal world.
2. **“A Contribution to the Psychogenesis of Manic-Depressive States”** (1935) — depressive position, ambivalence, loss, concern, and reparation.
3. **“Mourning and Its Relation to Manic-Depressive States”** (1940) — mourning as a reworking of internal object relations rather than simple detachment.
4. **“Notes on Some Schizoid Mechanisms”** (1946) — paranoid-schizoid position, splitting, projection, idealization, and projective identification.
5. **_Envy and Gratitude_** (1957) — envy, gratitude, destructiveness, the good object, and attacks on dependence.
6. **_Love, Guilt and Reparation and Other Works 1921–1945_** — the central early papers collected together.

## Secondary core

- **“The Importance of Symbol-Formation in the Development of the Ego”** (1930).
- **“Love, Guilt and Reparation”** (1937).
- **“On the Theory of Anxiety and Guilt”** (1948).
- **“Our Adult World and Its Roots in Infancy”** (1959).

## Branch question

Use Klein here to test whether love and hate coexist toward the same object, whether splitting is simplifying an unbearable ambivalence, and whether guilt can lead either toward reparation or toward defensive attack.
