# Canonical primary texts — Jacques Derrida

Use these as the primary textual shelf for this branch. Prefer authoritative editions, library copies, publisher-hosted material, or legitimately open texts. Do not vendor copyrighted scans merely because they circulate online.

## Core sequence

1. **_Of Grammatology_** (1967) — especially the opening critique of logocentrism and the treatment of writing, supplementarity, and différance.
2. **_Writing and Difference_** (1967) — especially “Structure, Sign, and Play in the Discourse of the Human Sciences” and “Violence and Metaphysics.”
3. **_Speech and Phenomena_** (1967) — Derrida’s compact confrontation with Husserl on presence, voice, repetition, and self-relation.
4. **_Dissemination_** (1972) — especially “Plato’s Pharmacy,” for supplement, pharmakon, undecidability, and the instability of binary hierarchy.
5. **_Margins of Philosophy_** (1972) — especially “Différance” and “Signature Event Context.”
6. **_Limited Inc_** (1977/1988) — useful for iterability, context, speech acts, and the consequences of repeatability.

## Secondary core

- **_Glas_** (1974) — Hegel, Genet, textual architecture, contamination of conceptual oppositions.
- **_The Post Card_** (1980) — transmission, address, delay, psychoanalysis, and the instability of sender/receiver positions.
- **_Specters of Marx_** (1993) — inheritance, haunting, debt, justice, and historical remainder.
- **“Force of Law: The ‘Mystical Foundation of Authority’”** (1990) — law, justice, decision, and undecidability.

## Branch question

Use Derrida here to test whether an opposition depends on what it excludes, whether a concept carries a trace of its contrary, and whether a supposedly complete structure is stabilized by something it cannot fully contain.
