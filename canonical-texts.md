# Canonical primary texts — René Girard

Use these as the primary textual shelf for this branch. Keep the early theory of mimetic desire distinct from the later, broader theory of sacrifice and scapegoating.

## Core sequence

1. **_Deceit, Desire and the Novel_** (1961) — triangular or mimetic desire: subjects learn what to desire through models and rivals.
2. **_Violence and the Sacred_** (1972) — rivalry, reciprocal violence, sacrifice, ritual, and the scapegoat mechanism.
3. **_Things Hidden Since the Foundation of the World_** (1978) — the broad synthesis of mimetic desire, scapegoating, religion, and biblical interpretation.
4. **_The Scapegoat_** (1982) — persecution texts, collective accusation, victim selection, and the concealment of collective violence.
5. **_A Theater of Envy: William Shakespeare_** (1991) — literary analysis of mimetic rivalry, jealousy, and prestige.
6. **_I See Satan Fall Like Lightning_** (1999) — mature presentation of mimetic theory and its Christian interpretation.

## Secondary core

- **_Resurrection from the Underground_** — Dostoevsky and mimetic desire.
- **_Battling to the End_** (2007) — Clausewitz, escalation, rivalry, and modern violence.

## Branch question

Use Girard here to test whether desire is borrowed from a model, whether rivalry intensifies because two people increasingly resemble one another, and whether a group restores cohesion by converging on a victim.
