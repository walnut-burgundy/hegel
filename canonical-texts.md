# Canonical primary texts — Theodor W. Adorno

Use these as the primary textual shelf for this branch. The repository already contains a working note on “Aspects of Hegel’s Philosophy.” Prefer authoritative editions and legitimate access sources.

## Core sequence

1. **_Hegel: Three Studies_** (1963) — especially “Aspects of Hegel’s Philosophy,” “The Experiential Content of Hegel’s Philosophy,” and “Skoteinos, or How to Read Hegel.”
2. **_Negative Dialectics_** (1966) — the central text for nonidentity, determinate negation, conceptual violence, and resistance to premature reconciliation.
3. **_Minima Moralia: Reflections from Damaged Life_** (1951) — philosophy at the scale of everyday experience, damaged subjectivity, exchange, intimacy, and survival.
4. **_Dialectic of Enlightenment_** (1944/1947), with Max Horkheimer — reason, domination, culture industry, myth, and self-undermining enlightenment.
5. **_Aesthetic Theory_** (posthumous, 1970) — autonomy, social truth-content, form, suffering, and the artwork’s resistance to identity-thinking.

## Secondary core

- **“The Essay as Form”** — method, non-systematic thinking, and resistance to exhaustive conceptual closure.
- **“Cultural Criticism and Society”** — immanent criticism and the entanglement of culture with domination.
- **“Commitment”** — political art, suffering, and the limits of didactic representation.
- **_The Authoritarian Personality_** (1950, collaborative) — empirical social psychology, authority, prejudice, and character structure.

## Branch question

Use Adorno here to test what a clean concept leaves out, what suffering falsifies an apparently reconciled system, and how social forms enter the most private parts of experience.
