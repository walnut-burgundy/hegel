# Canonical primary texts — Sigmund Freud

Use these as the primary textual shelf for this branch. Freud’s translations differ materially; record edition and translator when making close textual claims.

## Core sequence

1. **_The Interpretation of Dreams_** (1900) — dream-work, condensation, displacement, wish, censorship, and the logic of unconscious formation.
2. **_Three Essays on the Theory of Sexuality_** (1905) — sexuality, development, partial drives, and the instability of supposedly natural objects.
3. **“Mourning and Melancholia”** (1917) — loss, identification, self-reproach, ambivalence, and the internalization of the lost object.
4. **_Beyond the Pleasure Principle_** (1920) — repetition compulsion, trauma, drive, and behavior that cannot be reduced to pleasure maximization.
5. **_The Ego and the Id_** (1923) — structural conflict among ego, id, and superego; identification and unconscious ego processes.
6. **_Civilization and Its Discontents_** (1930) — aggression, guilt, renunciation, social order, and the cost of civilization.

## Secondary core

- **“Remembering, Repeating and Working-Through”** (1914).
- **“Repression”** (1915).
- **“Instincts and Their Vicissitudes” / “Drives and Their Fates”** (1915).
- **“The Uncanny”** (1919).
- **_Group Psychology and the Analysis of the Ego_** (1921).
- **_Inhibitions, Symptoms and Anxiety_** (1926).

## Branch question

Use Freud here to test whether a symptom is a compromise rather than an error, whether repetition outruns conscious intention, and whether love, hostility, guilt, loss, and self-punishment are mixed rather than cleanly separable.
