# Canonical primary texts — Wilfred R. Bion

Use these as the primary textual shelf for this branch. Bion’s technical vocabulary changes across periods; avoid treating later notation as a simple dictionary for earlier clinical writing.

## Core sequence

1. **_Experiences in Groups_** (1961; papers from the 1940s) — work groups, basic assumptions, dependency, fight-flight, pairing, and group mentality.
2. **_Learning from Experience_** (1962) — alpha function, beta elements, containment, thinking, frustration, and the transformation of raw experience into something thinkable.
3. **_Elements of Psycho-Analysis_** (1963) — the grid, elements of psychoanalysis, abstraction, and attempts to discipline psychoanalytic observation.
4. **_Transformations_** (1965) — transformations in knowledge, hallucinosis, invariants, and changes between experience and representation.
5. **_Attention and Interpretation_** (1970) — memory, desire, knowledge, “O,” and the analyst’s relation to what cannot be mastered in advance.
6. **_Second Thoughts_** (1967) — revised earlier papers, including work on psychosis, language, and thinking.

## Secondary core

- **_Cogitations_** (posthumous notes).
- **_A Memoir of the Future_** trilogy.
- Papers on attacks on linking and differentiation of psychotic/non-psychotic parts of personality.

## Branch question

Use Bion here to test whether an experience has become thinkable at all, whether unbearable material is being expelled rather than represented, whether a relationship can contain uncertainty, and whether apparent irrationality is occurring before ordinary reasoning can even begin.
