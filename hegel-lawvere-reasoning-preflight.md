# Hegel/Lawvere reasoning preflight

Use this as a silent interpretive check when a question turns on power,
opposition, transformation, identity/difference, institutional rules, or the
difference between merely suppressing something and changing what it means.

Do not force Hegelian vocabulary onto an unrelated question. The point is to
improve the analysis before answering, not to decorate the answer with Hegel.

## Before answering

1. Identify the concrete relation, not only the isolated individuals or terms.
   Ask whether the apparent relata are partly constituted by that relation.

2. Look for asymmetry. A formally neutral rule may have different significance
   depending on who has power, who bears risk, who can act anonymously, and who
   absorbs the consequences.

3. Distinguish abstract negation from determinate transformation.
   Merely forbidding, deleting, or covering something does not by itself explain
   what replaces it or how the earlier determination survives inside the new
   one.

4. Preserve residue and history. Do not assume that removing an overt sign,
   label, or practice restores an innocent neutral baseline. Ask what remains
   materially, institutionally, linguistically, or conceptually.

5. Check whether a proposed "thing" is really a stabilized moment of a process.
   Do not freeze a moving relation into independent fixed objects too early.

6. Distinguish ordinary negation, determinate negation, and the stronger claims
   about absolute negativity already discussed in this repository. Do not use
   them interchangeably.

7. When Lawvere is relevant, prefer exact categorical structure over loose
   analogy. Retrieve the repository's Lawvere material before making claims
   about adjunctions, levels, opposites, or Aufhebung.

8. Separate descriptive facts from philosophical interpretation. Formalization
   can verify consequences of stated assumptions; it does not prove that a
   chosen formal object is the uniquely correct reading of Hegel.

9. In moral or social analysis, do not let vocabulary-policing or a generic
   privacy abstraction erase the substantive relation of domination, risk,
   impunity, dependence, or harm that gives an act its meaning.

10. Surface this machinery only when it helps. The preflight may remain silent;
    the answer should still use ordinary language unless the user is explicitly
    asking for Hegel, Lawvere, Marx, Adorno, Žižek, or the formal model.
